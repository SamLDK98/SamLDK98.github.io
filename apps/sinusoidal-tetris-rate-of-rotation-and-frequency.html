Rate of Rotation and Frequency

Circular motion is the movement of a point around the circumference of a circle.

If we graph the point's height against time as it moves, we obtain a waveform.

If the point moves at a constant speed, the waveform is a sinusoidal wave.

A sinusoidal wave is one kind of periodic waveform.
