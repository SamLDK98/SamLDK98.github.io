(a) The two 45° angles make the legs equal, so AC = BC = 1. Pythagoras gives the hypotenuse: AB² = 1² + 1² = 2, so AB = √2.

(b) Mirror the triangle across XZ and the two 60° corners build an equilateral triangle — so the hypotenuse is twice the short leg: XY = 2. Pythagoras then gives XZ² = 2² − 1² = 3, so XZ = √3.
