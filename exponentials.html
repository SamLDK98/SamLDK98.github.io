Exponentials

Our overarching goal is to understand Euler's identity:

eiπ + 1 = 0

To get there, we first need to develop an understanding of what it means to raise a number to different types of exponents:

  • A positive integer.
  • Zero.
  • A negative integer.
  • A fraction.
  • An irrational number.
  • An imaginary number.

We will also explore what makes the mathematical constant e so significant.

Ultimately, we want to understand how these ideas come together in Euler's identity, and why this result reveals a remarkable connection between exponentiation and circular motion.

Two concepts are particularly important to understand along the way: multiplicative growth and fractional exponents.

Developing a strong intuition for these will provide a foundation for understanding much of what follows.
